## 💡 핵심 표현

- **work out** [¶4](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶4) — "알아내다, 해결하다." `figure out`보다 격식 있는 느낌으로, 문제·구조·답을 단계적으로 풀어낼 때 자주 쓴다.
- **be described as** [¶7](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶7) — "~로 묘사되다, ~로 평가되다." 평가나 평판을 객관적으로 전달할 때 쓰는 격식 표현.
- **for one** [¶8](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶8) — "우선 한 가지로, 한 예를 들면." 여러 근거 중 첫 번째를 제시할 때 쓰는 담화 표지.
- **yet to + 동사원형** [¶10](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶10) — "아직 ~하지 않은." `had yet to publish`는 "아직 발표하지 않았던"이라는 미완료의 의미를 담는다.
- **important enough to + 동사** [¶12](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶12) — "~할 만큼 중요한." `enough to` 구문은 "~하기에 충분한"이라는 정도·자격을 표현한다.
- **pass from one generation to the next** [¶13](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶13) — "한 세대에서 다음 세대로 전달되다." 유전·전통·문화의 세습을 묘사하는 관용 표현.

## 🗣️ 유용한 표현

- **It was ~ that ...** [¶1](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶1)
  - 뜻: "~한 것은 바로 …이었다" — 특정 정보를 강조하는 분열문(cleft sentence) 구조.
  - 본문: "It was in a pub named The Eagle, in the center of Cambridge in the UK, that one lunchtime in 1953 a scientist announced he had 'found the secret of life.'"
  - 활용: It was in Tokyo that I first tried sushi. / It was her perseverance that made the difference.

- **may have been** [¶2](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶2)
  - 뜻: "~였을지도 모른다" — 과거 사실에 대한 추측을 나타내는 조동사 완료형.
  - 본문: "But it may have been the most important."
  - 활용: That may have been the best decision she ever made. / It may have been a mistake to trust him.

- **although ~ said** [¶3](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶3)
  - 뜻: "비록 ~라고 말했지만" — 양보를 나타내는 접속사로 앞 내용에 단서나 반전을 추가.
  - 본문: "although he later said he didn't remember that moment"
  - 활용: Although she later said it was fine, I could tell she was upset.

- **have been doing** [¶6](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶6)
  - 뜻: "~해오고 있었다" — 과거완료 진행형으로 과거 시점까지 계속된 행동을 표현.
  - 본문: "Crick and Watson had been studying genes at the Cavendish Laboratory"
  - 활용: They had been working on the project for two years before it was canceled.

- **This has helped in** [¶14](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶14)
  - 뜻: "이것은 ~에 도움이 되어 왔다" — 현재완료로 지속적인 기여·효과를 나타내는 패턴.
  - 본문: "This has helped in things like forensic science, treating diseases, identifying human remains"
  - 활용: This research has helped in developing new treatments for cancer.

- **what it is** [¶5](../contents/2026-05-04_dna-discovery-secret-life.md#¶5)
