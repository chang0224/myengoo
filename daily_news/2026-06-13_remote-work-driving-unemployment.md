## 🗣️ 유용한 표현

- **may be driving ~** [¶1](#¶1)
  - 뜻: ~을 유발하고 있을 수도 있다. 원인이 불확실할 때 조심스럽게 주장하는 패턴.
  - 본문: "Remote Work, Not AI, May Be Driving Unemployment"
  - 활용: "Poor sleep may be driving your lack of focus at work."

- **As a result,** [¶3](#¶3)
  - 뜻: 결과적으로. 앞에서 언급한 원인의 결과를 이어 설명할 때 쓰는 담화 표지.
  - 본문: "As a result, there is a notably higher unemployment rate for younger workers in remotable occupations compared with older workers."
  - 활용: "The project was delayed. As a result, the launch was pushed back by two months."

- **compared with ~** [¶3](#¶3)
  - 뜻: ~와 비교하여. 두 대상의 차이를 나타낼 때 쓰는 패턴.
  - 본문: "there is a notably higher unemployment rate for younger workers in remotable occupations compared with older workers"
  - 활용: "Sales this quarter are up 15% compared with the same period last year."

- **Once ~, S + V** [¶8](#¶8)
  - 뜻: ~하자마자, ~하고 나서. 어떤 조건이 충족된 직후 일어나는 변화를 설명하는 패턴.
  - 본문: "Once its offices reopened, the company shifted back to hiring younger workers."
  - 활용: "Once you sign the contract, the process will move quickly."

- **even after ~** [¶8](#¶8)
  - 뜻: ~한 후에도. 변화 이후에도 지속되는 상황을 강조하는 패턴.
  - 본문: "But even after the reopening, the company favored more experienced workers for teams that included remote work."
  - 활용: "Even after the apology, trust was hard to rebuild."

- **in areas such as ~** [¶9](#¶9)
  - 뜻: ~과 같은 분야에서. 구체적인 예를 들어 범위를 설명할 때 쓰는 패턴.
  - 본문: "entry-level white-collar jobs in areas such as finance, law and media"
  - 활용: "There is strong demand for workers in areas such as data science and cybersecurity."

- **the study notes that ~** [¶10](#¶10)
  - 뜻: 연구는 ~을 지적한다. 연구·보고서의 내용을 인용할 때 쓰는 격식체 패턴.
  - 본문: "the study notes that the employment situation for young college graduates was getting worse before the development of AI tools"
  - 활용: "The report notes that consumer confidence has declined for the third consecutive month."
