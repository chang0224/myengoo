## 🗣️ 유용한 표현

- **For some ~, but for others ~** [¶1](#¶1)
  - 뜻: 어떤 사람들에게는 ~이지만, 다른 사람들에게는 ~ (두 집단을 대조할 때)
  - 본문: "**For some people,** AI is already part of their daily work routine. **But for others,** it's still something they rarely use."
  - 활용: For some employees, the new system is easy to use, but for others, it's been a real challenge.

- **This may mean ~** [¶4](#¶4)
  - 뜻: 이것은 ~를 의미할 수 있다 (데이터나 상황을 해석할 때)
  - 본문: "**This may mean** many people are still hesitant about using AI at work — but why?"
  - 활용: Sales dropped last quarter. This may mean we need to reconsider our pricing strategy.

- **not sure how to ~** [¶5](#¶5)
  - 뜻: ~하는 방법을 모르다, ~하는 것이 불확실하다
  - 본문: "people are **not sure how to** use AI in their daily tasks"
  - 활용: Many new employees are not sure how to navigate the internal approval process.

- **some said ~ while others said ~** [¶6](#¶6)
  - 뜻: 어떤 사람들은 ~라고 했고, 다른 사람들은 ~라고 했다 (복수 의견 대조)
  - 본문: "**some said** they've tried but don't find it helpful, **while others said** they don't feel prepared to use it."
  - 활용: Some participants said the training was useful, while others said it was too basic.

- **Although ~, ~ could still be enough to ~** [¶8](#¶8)
  - 뜻: 비록 ~이지만, 그래도 ~하기에 충분할 수 있다 (역접 후 여전한 영향력 강조)
  - 본문: "**Although** the study found that fewer than one in 10 workers said AI has caused problems in their work, this **could still be enough to** make others want to rely on their own skills instead."
  - 활용: Although the risk is small, it could still be enough to delay the project launch.

- **it looks like ~** [¶10](#¶10)
  - 뜻: ~인 것처럼 보인다 (확신은 아니지만 외양상 추측)
  - 본문: "**it looks like** not everyone is ready to use it just yet"
  - 활용: It looks like the meeting has been pushed back to next week.

- **no longer exist** [¶9](#¶9)
  - 뜻: 더 이상 존재하지 않다, 없어지다
  - 본문: "their job will **no longer exist** in the next five years because of AI"
  - 활용: Several of those roles may no longer exist once automation is fully implemented.
