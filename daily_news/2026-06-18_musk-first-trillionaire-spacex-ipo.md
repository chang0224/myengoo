## 📚 B2+ 단어장

- 본문 [¶1](#¶1): "shares of his rocket company SpaceX **soared** in Wall Street's biggest initial public offering"
- 예문: Tech stocks soared after the company announced record profits.

**whip up** /wɪp ʌp/ [구동사] (열광·감정을) 불러일으키다, 자극하다
- 본문 [¶2](#¶2): "Musk was able to **whip up** enthusiasm for the IPO"
- 예문: The coach whipped up excitement among the fans before the final match.

**holdings** /ˈhoʊldɪŋz/ [명사] 보유 지분, 보유 자산
- 본문 [¶5](#¶5): "Between his **holdings** in SpaceX and Tesla"
- 예문: She diversified her holdings across several industries to reduce risk.

**orbital** /ˈɔːrbɪtl/ [형용사] 궤도의

- 예문: The satellite entered its orbital path around the Earth successfully.

- 본문 [¶7](#¶7): "it needs money to fund its **lofty** ambitions"
