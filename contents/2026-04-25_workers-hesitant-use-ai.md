# Many Workers Still Hesitant to Use AI, Study Says

> 출처: (Engoo Daily News)
> 저장일: 2026-04-25
> 📖 [직독직해](../daily_news/2026-04-25_workers-hesitant-use-ai.md) · 📚 [단어장](../words/2026-04-25_workers-hesitant-use-ai.md)

---

#### ¶1

For some people, AI is already part of their daily work routine. They use it to write emails, find information or just save time.

#### ¶2

But for others, it's still something they rarely use.

#### ¶3

A recent Gallup study shows that about half of workers in the US now use AI at least a few times a year. But half said they only use it once a year — or not at all!

#### ¶4

This may mean many people are still hesitant about using AI at work — but why?

#### ¶5

One reason may be that people are not sure how to use AI in their daily tasks. The study found that even among AI users, regular use is not very high — only about 13% use it every day, while around 28% use it a few times a week or more.

#### ¶6

Among people who never use AI for work, some said they've tried but don't find it helpful, while others said they don't feel prepared to use it.

#### ¶7

Another reason people may be hesitant about using AI is trust.

#### ¶8

Some people worry that AI can give wrong answers or make mistakes. Although the study found that fewer than one in 10 workers said AI has caused problems in their work, this could still be enough to make others want to rely on their own skills instead.

#### ¶9

There are also concerns about job security, with 18% of workers saying they believe their job will no longer exist in the next five years because of AI. And in companies that already use AI, this number increases to 23%.

#### ¶10

AI might be becoming more common, but it looks like not everyone is ready to use it just yet.
