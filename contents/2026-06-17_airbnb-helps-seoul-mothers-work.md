# Airbnb Program Helps Seoul Mothers Return to Work

> 출처: https://engoo.co.kr/app/daily-news/article/airbnb-program-helps-seoul-mothers-return-to-work/4RXdjl2XEfGdM3tDVwDgJw

---

#### ¶1

South Korea has one of the lowest birth rates in the world. One of the reasons for this is that South Korean women often feel that starting a family could make it harder for them to build their careers.

#### ¶2

And mothers sometimes find it difficult to return to work after having children.

#### ¶3

But since 2024, the Seoul Foundation of Women & Family has been trying to change that — by teaching women how to make money on sites like Airbnb by hosting tourists in rental properties.

#### ¶4

The foundation has been running a training program aimed at "career-interrupted" women. In other words, women who have left work to have children or look after family members.

#### ¶5

The idea is that the program can help them to work in a flexible way that doesn't affect their family lives.

#### ¶6

The program is now in its third year, and has been working officially with Airbnb since 2025, as well as the Seoul Women's Resources Development Center.

#### ¶7

It has been popular since the start. The Korea Times said that in 2024, 130 people applied for 36 places on the five-week program. In 2025, 471 people applied.

#### ¶8

The online program teaches people about things like listing properties, pricing and tourism trends.

#### ¶9

Since taking part in the program, a number of women have begun their own hosting businesses.

#### ¶10

Kim Mi-ae started hosting in her 50s when her children had grown up. She said it wasn't easy at the start but she has worked hard. She hosts people in an apartment near Aeogae station in Seoul.

#### ¶11

The program is not only good for women in Seoul, it's also good for Airbnb. And The Korea Times said it's good for Seoul too, because there are not enough hotel rooms in the capital even as tourist numbers continue to increase.
