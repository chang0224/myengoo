# Two UK Pubs a Day Closed in Early 2026

#### ¶1

Pubs have long been at the center of British social and cultural life. Not just somewhere to eat and drink, they provide a "third space" outside the home or work where people can spend time with their community.

#### ¶2

However, in the first three months of 2026, a total of 161 pubs across the UK closed down — around two per day.

#### ¶3

This is according to the British Beer and Pub Association (BBPA), which also says these closures represent a loss of around 2,400 jobs. The number of closures is also a 26% increase compared to the same period in 2025.

#### ¶4

At the same time, British people are drinking less. According to a report published in the Financial Times, the average UK adult drank around 10 alcoholic drinks a week in 2024 — a quarter less than in 1990, and the lowest in 14 years.

#### ¶5

Marten Lodewijks from research company IWSR said this may be because the British population is getting older, meaning they aren't able to drink as much. It may also be because people are becoming more health conscious, while at the same time many just can't afford to go out and drink so much.

#### ¶6

Like a lot of countries, the UK is experiencing a cost-of-living crisis. Rising prices on essential things like food and electricity mean people have less money to spend on other things.

#### ¶7

However, the BBPA doesn't think the problem facing British pubs is just that people aren't coming through their doors and buying pints. CEO Emma McClarkin says pubs are doing a "brisk trade," but aren't earning enough in profits because they have to pay high taxes.

#### ¶8

According to the BBPA's calculations, for every three pounds spent in a pub, one pound is paid in taxes — where one pound is about $1.40. It wants the British government to come up with a plan to lower the costs of running a pub so that fewer have to close.
