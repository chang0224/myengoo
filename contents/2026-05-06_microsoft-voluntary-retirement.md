# Microsoft Offers Thousands of Staff Voluntary Retirement

> 출처: [Engoo Daily News](https://engoo.com/app/daily-news/article/microsoft-offers-thousands-of-staff-voluntary-retirement/0N3rVELWEfG9INNaDqzsag)

> 📖 [직독직해](../daily_news/2026-05-06_microsoft-voluntary-retirement.md) · 📚 [단어장](../words/2026-05-06_microsoft-voluntary-retirement.md)

---

#### ¶1

Microsoft is offering voluntary retirement to thousands of workers, as part of efforts to reduce staff while increasing spending on AI.

#### ¶2

Around 7% of the company's US workers will be eligible, according to CNBC. That's almost 9,000 workers out of the company's 125,000 US employees.

#### ¶3

The program — which is a first for Microsoft — is open to employees at the senior director level and below whose age and years at the company add up to at least 70.

#### ¶4

Microsoft said employees will be told on May 7 if they are eligible, and they will have 30 days to decide if they want to accept.

#### ¶5

The move comes as more tech companies are making staff cuts. One of the big reasons for this is the impact of AI, which is changing the way these companies work.

#### ¶6

Microsoft, which laid off about 9,000 workers in the summer of 2025, has been focusing more of its spending on data centers to support and improve its AI services. In the last few months of 2025, it spent $37.5 billion on data centers, according to CNN.

#### ¶7

Other tech companies, like Amazon and Alphabet, are doing the same.

#### ¶8

Amy Coleman from Microsoft said many of the employees eligible for the retirement offer have spent years, and sometimes decades, helping Microsoft become what it is today.

#### ¶9

She added that the company hopes this program will help workers take the next step in their lives while being supported by Microsoft.
