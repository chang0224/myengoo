# Is AI Making People Retire Early?

---

#### ¶1

Older workers may be encouraged to retire early by the rise of AI in the workplace.

#### ¶2

That's the view of retirement expert Robert Laura. Speaking to The Wall Street Journal, he said that people tend to retire when big parts of their work change.

#### ¶3

These changes could be things like colleagues leaving or retiring, the company changing its direction, or a feeling of losing autonomy. Or, it could be the rise of AI, Laura says.

#### ¶4

Data from the US has found that there are fewer workers aged over 55 in the US than ever before.

#### ¶5

There's no way of knowing how many people are retiring because of AI, but some older workers have shared their stories with The Wall Street Journal.

#### ¶6

A 68-year-old named Luke Michel told the newspaper that he'd learned about computers and the internet in the 1980s and 1990s. But he said doing all that again with AI "wasn't worth it" — so he retired.

#### ¶7

Taking time to learn something new in the later part of your career isn't something that everyone wants to do.

#### ¶8

IT worker Terry Grimm retired aged 65, he told The Wall Street Journal. He wanted to work longer, but when his company began encouraging staff to use AI, he walked away.

#### ¶9

He said that working for 40 hours a week and studying AI for another 20 hours was just too much.

#### ¶10

However, experts say it's never too late to learn — and that includes learning about AI. In fact, spending time learning later in life can be really good for brain health!
